# Common print product dimensions (verify against regional/printer-specific standards before finalizing)

| Product | Common size (US) | Common size (ISO/most other regions) |
|---|---|---|
| Business card | 3.5 x 2 in | 85 x 55 mm |
| Postcard | 4 x 6 in | A6 (105 x 148 mm) |
| Flyer | 8.5 x 11 in (Letter) | A4 (210 x 297 mm) |
| Trifold brochure | 8.5 x 11 in, folded to 3 panels | A4, folded to 3 panels |
| Poster | 18 x 24 in / 24 x 36 in | A2 / A1 |

Bleed: 0.125in (3mm) standard for most commercial printers — always confirm with the specific print vendor, since some require more (especially for die-cut or unusually shaped pieces).

Packaging dielines are never standardized the way paper sizes are — always obtain the actual dieline template from the box/label manufacturer being used rather than assuming standard dimensions.
